A Manchester story

Rooted in the city.
Made for the game.

Manchester City began in 1880 as St Mark's, a football team formed by members of a church in Gorton. The club grew with its city, taking the name Manchester City in 1894 and building generations of supporters along the way.

Today, sky blue remains the unmistakable thread connecting the club's beginnings to every new matchday. The badge may change; the sense of belonging does not.

“This is our city. This is our club.”
